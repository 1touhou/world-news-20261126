# World News — 26 November 2026

A code-drawn 1024×1024 newspaper infographic of six world stories from
Thursday, 26 November 2026:

- **North Korea** — Kim Jong Un calls for tactical nuclear weapons to be
  deployed more widely; KCNA says he inspected nuclear-material production.
- **Trade** — the Trump administration proposes new tariffs on imports from
  14 trading partners, including China, India and the EU.
- **Middle East** — Yemen's Houthis say they struck the Saudi ports of
  Jeddah and Jazan, and claim earlier strikes on Riyadh.
- **Egypt** — 31 people are killed after a petroleum vehicle overturns and
  catches fire on a major ring road.
- **USA** — New York postpones the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade to
  Saturday as a major storm is forecast.
- **Music** — Andy Taylor, guitarist of Duran Duran, dies aged 65.

The image (`entry.png`) is pre-rendered and checked in. `main.py` is a
Python 3 standard-library-only program: it copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). No third-party
packages, no network, no fonts. The copy is verified by comparing file
sizes before the program exits.

Built for the make.fun news-image-20261126 bounty.
